# App Facts Template

Use this template before asking AI to write landing-page, privacy, support, or App Store content.

The template gives you a consistent place to record what the app actually does. It does not contain launch copy or answers. Complete it from the finished app, the codebase, and information confirmed by the appropriate developer, product owner, or organization.

## Status values

Use one status for every fact:

- **Verified in app or code:** You confirmed the behavior directly.
- **Verified by owner:** The appropriate developer, product owner, or organization confirmed it.
- **Unknown:** The information still needs confirmation.
- **Not applicable:** The item does not apply to this app or release.

Do not use an unknown item as a product or privacy claim. Add more fact records when needed, and keep each ID unique.
