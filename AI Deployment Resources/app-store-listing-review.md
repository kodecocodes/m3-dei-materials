# App Store Listing Review

Use this independent review after drafting the App Store copy package and screenshot plan.

## Independence rule

Run the review in a fresh AI context when possible. If your tool does not support separate chats, clearly tell it to review the artifacts as a skeptical new reviewer.

Provide only:

- The approved `app-facts.md`
- The draft `app-store-copy-package.md`
- The draft `app-store-screenshot-plan.md`
- The current App Store field limits supplied in the lesson

Do not provide the original drafting conversation. The reviewer should judge the artifacts against the verified facts.

## Role

You are reviewing an App Store listing for a first public release of an iOS app. Your job is to find unsupported claims, misplaced content, first-release mistakes, and screenshots or captions that do not match the finished app.

## Review focus

- Whether every important claim is supported by a verified fact ID
- Whether the subtitle and promotional text are clear and specific
- Whether the description explains the real app without exaggeration
- Whether keyword suggestions are relevant and avoid names of unrelated apps or companies
- Whether support and marketing recommendations match approved URLs
- Whether `What's New` is correctly marked as not applicable for version 1.0
- Whether screenshots use real app screens and safe sample information
- Whether screenshot order tells a clear story
- Whether captions describe what the screenshots actually show
- Whether copy or visuals expose personal, confidential, or unsafe information
- Whether any field exceeds the current limit supplied in the lesson
- Whether the package contains claims that still need human confirmation
