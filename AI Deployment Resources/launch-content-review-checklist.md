# Launch Content Review Checklist

Use this checklist after AI drafts the landing-page, privacy, and support materials. AI can help find inconsistencies, but the developer owns every published claim.

## Verify the source

- [ ] The draft was created from the current, human-reviewed `app-facts.md`.
- [ ] Every important feature, audience, privacy, and support claim references a verified fact ID.
- [ ] Unknown facts remain questions or placeholders rather than published claims.
- [ ] No AI response is treated as evidence that the app behaves a certain way.

## Review the landing-page copy

- [ ] The opening explains who the app is for and what it helps them do.
- [ ] The feature list describes behavior visible in the finished app.
- [ ] Benefits are reasonable and do not promise guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] The copy avoids unsupported superlatives such as `best`, `fastest`, or `guaranteed`.
- [ ] The page does not describe deferred, hidden, or planned features as available.
- [ ] Known limitations are not disguised by vague marketing language.
- [ ] The tone fits the intended audience.

## Review visual direction

- [ ] Every proposed visual uses a real app screen or approved product asset.
- [ ] Screenshots use fictional or safe sample information.
- [ ] Captions match what is actually visible.
- [ ] Decorative imagery cannot be mistaken for app functionality.
- [ ] AI-generated interfaces are not presented as screenshots of the finished app.

## Review privacy language

- [ ] Data-collection statements were confirmed by the appropriate owner.
- [ ] Third-party SDKs and partners are included when they handle data.
- [ ] The draft does not assume that code inspection proves every organizational practice.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, sharing, and privacy-choice statements are verified or clearly unresolved.
- [ ] The draft does not claim legal compliance merely because AI produced formal language.
- [ ] The file is still labeled as a draft until the appropriate organizational or legal review is complete.

## Review support information

- [ ] The support method is approved and monitored.
- [ ] URLs and contact information are accurate.
- [ ] Personal contact information is not used unless explicitly approved.
- [ ] The support copy does not promise response times or services the team cannot provide.

## Final decision

For every issue found, mark one:

- Accept and revise
- Reject
- Verify with an owner
- Defer until the information exists

Mark the package as one:

- Ready for the App Store copy lesson
- Ready with open questions
- Needs another fact-verification pass
