# Fictional Launch Feedback Dataset: Tokens Dashboard 1.0

This dataset is fictional and created for the Module 8 feedback-synthesis exercise. It contains no real customer information.

## Product context

Tokens Dashboard 1.0 is an iPhone app for team leads, engineering managers, and project leads. It uses local sample data to display:

- A summary of AI usage and delivery metrics
- Cost by model
- Tokens versus outcomes
- Ticket-to-merge trends
- Team-level AI Usage Insights generated by deterministic local rules

The AI Usage Insights screen shows review prompts for model concentration, model changes, delivery trends, and insufficient data. Each card includes a supporting metric, explanation, suggested next step, and caveat.

Important product constraints:

- Insight cards are team-level and do not show individual developer names.
- The app does not call an LLM at runtime.
- The app does not claim AI caused delivery improvements.
- The app does not classify models as premium, standard, or cheap.
- The workflow-pattern insight was deferred from version 1.0 because it could become an individual performance signal.
- Insight cards do not deep-link to supporting charts in version 1.0.
- Thresholds are fixed and cannot be configured in version 1.0.
- The app has no in-app analytics instrumentation in version 1.0.
